Antonis Ballis

Associate Professor in Financial Technology
Programme Director, MSc Financial Technology
Department of Accounting and Finance, Aston Business School, Aston University

My research is empirical and quantitative, and studies how new forms of money and digital assets interact with existing financial institutions. My work has appeared in Risk Analysis, the Journal of Financial Stability, the International Journal of Central Banking, the Journal of Futures Markets, the International Review of Financial Analysis and the International Journal of Finance & Economics, among others.

At Aston University I direct the MSc Financial Technology. I hold a PhD from the Athens University of Economics and Business and I am a Senior Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (SFHEA).

Research interests:

  • Cryptocurrencies and stablecoins
  • Central bank digital currencies (CBDCs)
  • Digital and cross-border payments
  • AI in financial markets
